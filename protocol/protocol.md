# Review Protocol

## Objective
Estimate the effect of SGLT2 inhibitors on cardiovascular and patient-reported outcomes in HFpEF.

## Eligibility criteria
**Include:** randomized controlled trials; adults with HFpEF; SGLT2 inhibitor vs placebo/usual care; follow-up >= 12 weeks; reporting at least one outcome of interest.

**Exclude:** observational studies, conference abstracts without data, HFrEF-only populations, duplicate publications (use the most complete report).

## Outcomes
- Primary: composite of cardiovascular death or first HF hospitalization
- Secondary: CV death; all-cause mortality; total HF hospitalizations; KCCQ-CSS change; serious adverse events

## Study selection
Two reviewers screen titles/abstracts, then full texts, independently. Disagreements are resolved by discussion or a third reviewer.

## Risk of bias
Cochrane RoB 2 tool, assessed per outcome by two reviewers.

## Synthesis
Random-effects meta-analysis (see `analysis/statistical_analysis_plan.md`). Heterogeneity via I^2 and tau^2. Certainty of evidence via GRADE.
