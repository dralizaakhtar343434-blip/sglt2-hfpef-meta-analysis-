# Search Strategy

Databases: PubMed/MEDLINE, Embase, Cochrane CENTRAL, ClinicalTrials.gov. Also check reference lists of included trials and relevant reviews.

## PubMed draft
```
("sodium-glucose transporter 2 inhibitors"[MeSH] OR SGLT2[tiab] OR empagliflozin[tiab] OR dapagliflozin[tiab] OR canagliflozin[tiab] OR sotagliflozin[tiab] OR ertugliflozin[tiab])
AND
("heart failure"[MeSH] OR "heart failure"[tiab])
AND
("preserved ejection fraction"[tiab] OR HFpEF[tiab] OR "diastolic heart failure"[tiab])
AND
(randomized controlled trial[pt] OR randomized[tiab] OR placebo[tiab])
```
Record the date of each search and the number of records retrieved.
