# SGLT2 Inhibitors in HFpEF: Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis

Systematic review and meta-analysis of sodium-glucose cotransporter-2 (SGLT2) inhibitors in heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF).

## Status
Protocol and templates stage. No results have been generated yet.

## Research question (PICO)
- **Population:** adults with HFpEF (LVEF >= 40-50%, per trial definition)
- **Intervention:** SGLT2 inhibitors (empagliflozin, dapagliflozin, canagliflozin, sotagliflozin, etc.)
- **Comparator:** placebo or standard care
- **Outcomes:** composite of CV death or HF hospitalization (primary); CV death, all-cause death, HF hospitalization, KCCQ score, adverse events (secondary)
